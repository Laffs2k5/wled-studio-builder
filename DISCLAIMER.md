passes can still be wrong for your device, and flashing any firmware can fail or be interrupted.
That can leave a device unable to start ("bricked"), erase or change its configuration, or cause
connected hardware, such as LED strips and power supplies, to behave unexpectedly or be damaged.
The gate's checks reduce some of these risks; they do not remove them, and they may themselves be
wrong.
